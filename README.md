#How to play:
The WASD, arrows or ZQSD and you can jump with the spacebar or else.

#Lore
One day you receive a strange letter in the mail, it’s nothing special, just another ad filling up your mailbox just like the others. But you can’t help but feel a strange curiosity and look further into it. “Playground”, an indoor children’s park nearby, but wasn’t this park closed years ago because of the many disappearances there ? Quite odd to get an ad for it now, curiosity gets the better of you and you decide to go check it out. When you get to the place you see “do not cross” signs all over the doors and missing person posters. It doesn’t stop you, the more you explore this place the more you notice its strange features, such as random spikes in some of the playrooms. Everytime fall or get harmed by the spikes you wake up at the start of the room with a plastic ball in your hand containing a piece of paper with new information about this odd place. Are you going to avoid getting harmed at all cost and get out of this place the quickest way possible? Or are you willing to wake up again and again to bounce back and get to know more about this peculiar location?
